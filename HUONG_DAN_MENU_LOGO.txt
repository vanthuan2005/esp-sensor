CAP NHAT MENU ACTIVITY
=====================

CHI SUA GIAO DIEN MENU:
- Xoa dong "CHOOSE GAME MODE".
- Gom icon ♛ va chu CHESS thanh 1 cum logo.
- Cum logo can giua ngang.
- Logo nam o phan tren, ngay phia tren khu vuc 2 button.
- 2 button PLAY WITH FRIEND / PLAY WITH BOT van giu o giua man hinh.
- Khong sua logic Intent hay cac activity khac.

CACH DUNG:
1. Mo MenuActivity.java hien tai.
2. Ctrl+A -> dan toan bo MenuActivity.java moi.
3. Neu package khac com.example.chessgame thi giu package cua project.
4. Build -> Make Project -> Run.
